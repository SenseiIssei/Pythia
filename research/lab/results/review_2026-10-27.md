# R0: a review of all of Pythia (2026-10-27)

Nine area reviews ran in parallel (data, research method, models and factories, strategies and money, pump.fun,
operations, the research factory, the outside view, the owner's experience). Each claim below was checked again
against the code or the data folder before it went in; what could not be checked says UNVERIFIED. `<data>` is the
lab's data folder. No code was changed for this review.

## Verdict: the five findings that matter most

1. **Two real-money caps can be switched off by a config line, and the docs still say they are fixed in code.**
   With `limits=wallet` the pump.fun test maxima (2 SOL total, 0.5 SOL a day, 0.25 a trade) stop applying
   (`lab/pumpfun_rt/live_exec.py:181-188`, `NO_LIMIT`). That is how the wallet was emptied. In the on-chain gate the
   alpha mode skips the paper proof (`lab/onchain/live_gate.py:404-405`), and `limits=wallet` makes the daily loss
   cap infinite (`live_gate.py:247-249`). The L1 report suggests exactly that pair for the arming file
   (`results/onchain_longterm_l1.md:103-115`). `SAFETY.md:87` and `research/ops/README.md:325` still say
   "0.05 SOL a trade (hard in code)".
2. **The pump.fun live bot had no edge. It should stay off.** 149 real trades over 39 hours lost 0.4766 SOL
   (`<data>/live/pumpfun_rt/ledger.csv`, `totals.json`, recomputed).
   - Per trade: mean -2.6 % (bootstrap 95 % band -8.4 % to +3.1 %), 49.7 % winners.
   - Three things killed it: stops the price fell through (18 trades below -40 % against a -20 % stop), stakes of
     100 % of the wallet with the drawdown brake off (`control.json`, 2026-10-23), and about 3 % of costs per round
     trip (2.3 % pool fee, 0.7 % network).
   - Paper made +5.9 % a trade on the same rule. The gap is mostly which coins each side bought: only 34 of 162
     paper coins were also bought live in one 24 h window.
3. **No strategy has evidence of an edge today, and the yardsticks need repair before more searching pays.**
   - **The test window is spent.** The 2024+ window has been looked at by every study since the families sweep and
     by every factory run (4 to 6 a run, F2 every day). It is now validation, not out of sample.
   - **Trial counts are fragmented.** The factory starts from 377 earlier trials (`lab/factory/ledger.py:43`), the
     director already counts 1,115 (`lab/experiments/director_common.py:23`), F2 starts at 16 and S1 counts 8 for a
     pick from 61 configurations.
   - **S2 inverts the p-value.** It reports 1 - DSR (`lab/experiments/smallaccount_s2.py:691`, lower is better),
     while every other report gives DSR (higher is better). tsmom1's "0.65" and F2's "0.31" sit side by side.
   - **tsmom1 is filled at the close its signal uses.** One close late, its out-of-sample Sharpe falls from 1.15 to
     0.96 and its in-sample Sharpe from 1.97 to 1.49 (reviewer's rerun on the server panel).
   - **Forward records cannot prove an edge.** Telling a true Sharpe of 1.0 from zero takes about 12 years at 80 %
     power; a 30-day record only catches bugs.
4. **Several failures are silent today.**
   - **Disk.** The forecast says 0.7 GB a day and 220 days left (`<data>/_ops/disk/latest.json`). Free space
     actually fell from 274 to 165 GB in 7 days (`history.jsonl`) while the tracked folders grew 5 GB. The same
     thing happened before 2026-10-16, when free space was 93 GB. The cause is `measure_disk`
     (`lab/ops/jobs.py:1196-1210`), which counts only the tracked folders.
   - **Health alerts.** The alert latches after the first post (`lab/ops/notify.py:2304-2311`). While local-llm keeps
     the system red, a new failure posts nothing.
   - **Backups.** They miss the engine state and the safe account (`<data>/_ops/backup/latest.json`: `server`,
     `costs`, `trials` missing), and they sit on the same disk.
   - **The local model.** It has been down since it was deployed (32 failed runs, exit 125), while `llm-news` reports
     ok after every call got HTTP 405 (`<data>/ai/local_llm.jsonl`).
5. **The self-improving loop spends more on rejected work than on accepted work.**
   - Of 18 lane tasks, 6 passed (`<data>/reports/research/latest.json`). 49.93 of 87.32 USD went to runs that were
     rejected after the work was done, most for "forbidden files changed": `research/ops/README.md` in 4 of 6.
   - The budget stands at -68.09 USD and is paused. The briefing hides that line whenever the balance is 0
     (`lab/ops/briefing.py:446`).
   - The lane runner, the task files and the budget gate live outside the repository.
   - An accepted lane commit (a9fb2b6, L1) changed real-money modules (`lab/onchain/live_exec.py`, `live_gate.py`)
     within two minutes of being written. Whether the owner reviewed it is UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Data

**Good.**
- The recorder covers 99.99 % of minutes.
- Funding for BTC, ETH and SOL is complete from 2020.
- Hourly and daily timing is correct: UTC days, last hourly close, days with fewer than 20 bars dropped.
- The panel keeps delisted pairs.
- The D1 overlap checks (Binance against Kraken and Yahoo) are a good habit.

**Wrong or risky.**
- **Futures metrics lose the last day of every month for good.** `research/recorder/recorder/backfill.py:160-190`
  builds the month file from the daily files present that night and never fetches it again. All 20
  `<data>/hist/binance_um/metrics/symbol=*/2026-09.parquet` end at 2026-09-29 23:55 (checked for XRPUSDT).
- **The perp cache the sweeps read is frozen and lets dead perps be shorted.**
  - `fam_common.panel_from` calls `_perp_daily()` without `fresh` (`lab/experiments/fam_common.py:256`), so
    `<data>/lab-cache/cache/perp_daily.parquet` ends 2026-10-06 (checked). After that, panels have no shortable
    perps and zero funding.
  - The cache holds 57,210 base-days with zero perp volume (checked). The reviewer found 7,478 of them on days the
    spot traded over 1M USD (FTT, RAY and 83 more), so studies could short a dead perp at zero funding in exactly
    the collapsing coins. That flatters every short-the-losers result (M4 books, families with short legs).
    `perps_neutral.py:127-151` works around it locally; the others do not.
- **Funding top-up only keeps `contractType == "PERPETUAL"`** (`backfill.py:358`). 222 perps, gold among them, have
  no October funding and are filled with 0 (the cause is inferred from the data).
- **The pre-2020 history on the server is thin.** `daily_pre2020.parquet` holds BTC and ETH only. The "99 pairs" of
  D1 came from a run elsewhere, and the full fetch first happens inside next Sunday's weekly run with no
  completeness check.
- **Freshness is never checked.** The health check measures size, not age (`jobs.py:1219-1240`). `<data>/etf/daily`
  was last written 2026-10-13 and no job refreshes it. The briefing silently drops its ETF lines, and the small books
  depend on it. Pokemon misses two days that cannot be backfilled.
- **Point in time.**
  - The F2 universe (BTC, ETH, SOL, SPYx, QQQx, PAXG) is a 2026 choice tested from 2018.
  - Yahoo's adjusted closes are rewritten at each dividend.
  - Before 2020 the universe has USDT pairs only, while most altcoin volume then was against BTC.
  - `lab/ops/hurdles.py:34-37` reads FRED's latest revised values (fine for CPI as a hurdle, not for features).
- **Recorded and never read:** `binance_um/premium`, `open_interest`, `liquidations`, `kraken/trades`,
  `hist/binance_spot/klines_5m` (1.4 GB). They are cheap and some cannot be fetched later, so keep recording, but say
  so in the README.

**What to do.** Fix the month-end refetch, refresh the perp cache on every panel build, drop zero-volume perp bars
as not shortable, take every contract type in the funding top-up, add an age check per dataset, and add a
completeness check for the long history (all in brief R4). Missing free data that is not queued:
- Binance's delisting and monitoring announcement history (a point-in-time delist hazard);
- daily spot ETF flows;
- CFTC Commitments of Traders for CME bitcoin;
- historical market caps, to pick a universe by size at the time.

## 2. Research method

**Good.**
- `metrics.deflated_sharpe` (`lab/metrics.py:38-49`) matches Bailey and Lopez de Prado; the reviewer recomputed the
  published bars.
- The simulator earns a weight set at close t on day t+1, and Donchian levels exclude the current bar
  (`fam_common.py:315`).
- breakout_top10 reproduces: 1.16 / 1.05 against 1.20 / 1.08 published, on a panel two weeks longer.
- M4 enters one hour after the features with an embargo of horizon plus one day.
- The reports' verdicts are cautious and follow their numbers, with one exception: L1 after S2 (below).

**Wrong or risky.**
- **Trial counts** are spread over constants (377, 265, 183, 908, 1,115) and reset per mode (F2 16, S1 8). This
  undercounts by at least 738 trials in the factory's bar.
- **One p-value convention is inverted** (S2, above). The README still lists `tsmom` as "Candidate, deflated p 0.82",
  but that number came from 16 trials on today's 20 surviving coins.
- **The 2024+ window is no longer out of sample.** `results/factory_open_g1.md:60` and `:77` say outright that extra
  held-back looks were made and left out of the seed.
- **S1, S2 and D1 fill at the signal close** with no late-fill test (F2 has one).
- **Small-account costs come from one quote snapshot and one exchange rate.** Jupiter quotes from 2026-10-24 are
  applied back to 2018, and EUR/USD is fixed at 1.1194. SPYx is priced at SPY's close, which is 3 to 4 hours stale at
  the 00:00 UTC decision and stale all weekend.
- **L1 puts a modified tsmom1 on the wallet's code path although S2 said not to.** It runs BTC/ETH only, ETH at most
  25 %, with a 10 % band, and none of the changes is counted as a trial.
- **Minor points.**
  - The forward audit judges fill cost by an unweighted median (1.2 bps, while its 90th percentile is 30 bps).
  - M4's "t 47" ignores overlapping 3-day labels.
  - The README says "purged", but `cv.py` only embargoes.
  - `cv.py`, `deflated_sharpe` and `labels.py` have no unit tests.

**What to do.** One append-only trial registry, one DSR convention with its direction in every header, a new
lockbox, and a late-fill test for every small-account rule (briefs R5, R6, R10).

## 3. Models and factories

**Good.**
- The trial ledger only grows.
- G1 has a leak test that scrambles every bar after t.
- The zoo gates use Diebold-Mariano on per-day losses.
- F3's fill model uses measured latency, impact, failures and fees.

**Wrong or risky.**
- **The weekly factory bar is unreachable for the wrong reason.** The noise variance comes from in-sample trial
  Sharpes, which mostly reflect bull-market beta. At 2.68 over 1,577 trials, a genome needs a held-back Sharpe near
  3.7 (the reviewer's arithmetic); BTC's is 0.72. Deflating the excess over a benchmark is the honest form.
- **Near-clones get re-checked.** Only exact keys are blocked: on 2026-10-25 one strategy was sent to the held-back
  window four times. G1's canonical form misses equivalent trees (`btc > ma_100`, `z_100(btc) > 0` and two more give
  identical numbers under four keys).
- **The F3 bar is broken.** `lab/pumpfun_rt/factory.py:370-379` takes the variance over all trials, including the
  62 of 300 with no entries. The bar is 4.977 per entry (`<data>/reports/pumpfun_rt/factory.json`, checked), so
  nothing can pass.
- **F3 is scored on very few days.** Only three days are scored (2026-10-22 to 24), the two held-back days fall into
  the next night's search window, and the wallet bootstrap draws 30 days from 3. The briefing prints medians like
  0.55 SOL to 42.7 SOL in 30 days.
- **The zoo churns.** The champion swap is a fixed margin with no test (`lab/models/registry.py:167-172`). M8, M10,
  M11 and M12 fail their gates and are retrained anyway, and no book reads a zoo model today.
- **The A1 allocator draws at random although every arm runs as paper**, so every reward is seen anyway. Its own
  note says weighting would have made +112 % against +72.7 %. The posteriors ignore that the arms share 1,311
  entries, and the "deflated p 0.97" in its report reads backwards.
- **The rebalance band can lift a long-only book above 100 %** (`lab/factory/build.py:357-362`; the shadow CSV
  shows 1.027 to 1.031).
- **F2 lost its only survivor.** D1 moved the in-sample start, and `current()` (`lab/factory/small.py:875-877`)
  drops old-window rows, so the survivor whose held-back line the seed left out is never checked again (no shadow
  folder on the server).

**What to do.** Brief R6 (lockbox, benchmark-relative deflation, PBO, effective N) and brief R7 (F3 bar and day
clustering). Zoo: promote only on a test, stop retraining models that failed their gate until a book reads them.
Allocator: weight by posterior instead of drawing.

## 4. Strategies and money

**What the books show** (`<data>/reports`, 2026-10-26 and 27):
- small50_tsmom1 48.91 EUR (-2.19 %, all of it the cost of getting in);
- small100_breakout_sol3 98.71 (-1.29 %);
- small40_spy_trend +0.63 %;
- onchain_longterm 50.26 from 50.29 USDC, with its four real quotes at 1.85 to 2.31 bps as modelled;
- F2 2026-10-27: 136 trials, 0 survivors;
- forward report: tsmom, tsmom_regime and tsmom_top20 below their bands (one bet; the 2026-10-20 audit found them
  true to their rule);
- readiness: none ready;
- pumpfun_migration paper book: -99.45 % after 12 days.

**Good.**
- The swap costs, minimums, the SOL reserve and dust are modelled in detail and agree with the live quotes.
- S2's own conclusion ("an index fund is the safe answer") is honest.

**Wrong or risky.**
- **L1's arming path** (verdict 1).
- **The F2 benchmark rests on one bad point.** The SPYx premium noise contains a -1094 bps day drawn at random, and
  SPYx's drawdown over 2024 to 2026 moved from 18.6 % to 23.0 % between two runs. At 23 % the benchmark itself
  fails F2's 20 % gate.
- **The way back to EUR is never charged**, and S2 assumes the SOL is already in the wallet. Issuer, freeze and
  redemption risk of xStocks are listed but not priced. Whether a German resident may hold SPYx this way, and the
  taxes, are open.
- **"Beat holding SPYx" is the wrong bar.** For this owner the alternative is a UCITS index fund in EUR, and a
  crypto-only rule (tsmom1) should be judged against holding BTC and ETH.
- **The readiness check reads a capital change as a loss.** Auto-Pick showed a 49.97 % drawdown when 10,000 USD was
  moved out (2026-10-21 to 22).
- **Forward power is low.** F2's 30-day "beats SPYx" check passes 0.55 of the time with a true 0.5 Sharpe edge and
  0.50 with none.

**What to do.**
- Real money today: none on a strategy.
- If on-chain execution is to be learnt with real money, use at most 50 EUR in L1, after verdict 1 is fixed and 30
  clean paper days. Rules: a fixed cap, no top-ups for 90 days, stop on any reconciliation mismatch, on 2 failed
  swaps in a row, on costs above 2x the model over 10 swaps, or on more than 1 % drift from the paper twin. Judge it
  at 90 days on execution, not on return.
- Fix the readiness drawdown to use profit or cash-flow-adjusted equity.
- Use a daily EUR/USD series.
- Clean the premium series.

## 5. pump.fun

**What the record says.**
- **Trades and results.** 149 trades (7 P_cmig_model, 28 P_pool0, 114 P_pool0_wait), 2026-10-22 17:09 to
  2026-10-24 08:37 UTC. Average win +25.7 %, average loss -30.6 %. Turnover 30.6 SOL.
- **Costs.**
  - Network and priority fees: 0.110 SOL, about 0.7 % of a round trip.
  - Pool fees: about 1.2 % a side.
  - Buy slippage: median +3.1 %, against +1.4 % on paper.
- **Latency.** Signal to landed buy: median 6.2 s (paper assumes 1 s), mostly waiting for the creation event and the
  public stream's lag. Sales took a median 0.6 s.
- **Failed transactions.** 135 failed, mostly bonding-curve sales whose simulation failed, but they cost only
  0.004 SOL. The curve sell path was broken and left 2 positions unsold.
- **Sizing.** After the stake went to 100 % with the brake off, one trade lost 76 % of the wallet.
- **Removed limits.** Every limit still in force held. The removed ones would have stopped the bot at about
  -0.185 SOL on 2026-10-23, a day that lost 0.47 SOL.

**Should it come back?** No, not on this evidence.
- Showing a +3 % mean with this spread takes about 550 live trades.
- The losses come from tail gaps, sizing and the fee floor, none of which better coin selection removes.
- The F3 report's "0.55 SOL to 25 to 97 SOL in 30 days" is not credible next to the live record.

**Before any restart:**
- the caps made unconditional (R1);
- a share of 5 % or less, so that a -90 % trade costs a few % of the wallet;
- a fixed SOL loss budget instead of a stop rule that needs about 700 trades to fire;
- the stored all-in settings reset (`control.json`, `live_settings.json`);
- the container's `--restart unless-stopped` removed;
- paper reported with and without its top 1 to 2 % of trades, and compared only on coins both sides saw.

## 8. Outside view (free, 2025 and 2026)

Checked here:
- Binance's public archive has `bookDepth` for USD-M perps from 2023-01-01, a snapshot every 30 s at +-0.2 % and 1 to
  5 % from mid. Nothing in the repo uses it. It gives D3 three years of liquidity instead of weeks of own recording
  (see [binance-public-data](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data)).
- TabPFN 2.5 weights are non-commercial and non-production
  ([licence](https://huggingface.co/Prior-Labs/tabpfn_2_5/blob/main/LICENSE)).
- Rahimikia, Ni and Wang find off-the-shelf time-series foundation models weak in finance, zero-shot and fine-tuned
  ([arXiv 2511.18578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18578)); the exact R2 figures were not checked.

From the reviewer's search (links in the sources, not all re-read here):
- **Overfitting tests.** PBO by CSCV and effective trial counts are the standard complement to DSR. Newer DSR
  variants appear in [Lopez de Prado and Porcu 2025](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7198158).
  A deliberately leaky strategy passed both DSR and PBO in [arXiv 2608.27734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27734),
  which argues for a leak harness next to them.
- **Formal multiple comparisons.** `arch` has SPA, StepM and the model confidence set
  ([docs](https://bashtage.github.io/arch/multiple-comparison/multiple-comparison_examples.html)), for "beat a
  baseline" in the zoo and M4.
- **xStocks** publishes a keyless API with oracles, proof of reserves and corporate actions
  ([docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/apis/openapi)). Recording it daily is the only way to make it point in time.
  Tracking error rises with volatility, and weekend volume collapses
  ([FRL 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612326007294)).
- **Data for other venues and checks.**
  - [Tardis.dev](https://docs.tardis.dev/downloadable-csv-files/api) gives the first day of each month free, for fill
    checks.
  - [Kalshi](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/historical_data) has keyless historical data, as a second
    venue next to Polymarket.
  - A public pump.fun corpus of 798k launches ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jocry/Pumpfun_Memecoin_Corpus))
    could test Y17 outside our own days; its licence is ambiguous.
- **What has gone.** Dune's free tier is view-only, CoinGlass has no free API, Pyth's history now needs a key, and
  Flipside stopped exports (uncertain).
- **pump.fun papers.**
  - Only about 0.6 % of launches graduate ([arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14860)).
  - Over 82 % of high-return meme coins show manipulation ([arXiv 2507.01963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01963)).
  - Coordinated sniper groups are common ([arXiv 2607.02795](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02795)).
  - Sandwiches mostly hit wide-slippage pump.fun trades ([IMC 2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3730567.3764493)).

  All of this fits the live record.
- **LLM agents for alpha.** No result has been replicated independently. When every evaluation is counted as a
  trial, nothing survives ([arXiv 2605.16895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16895)).
- **Tools to borrow from.**
  - skfolio's `CombinatorialPurgedCV`, for a distribution of out-of-sample paths;
  - nautilus_trader's fill model, as a reference for the engine;
  - freqtrade's lookahead and recursive analysis ([docs](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/lookahead-analysis/)),
    as the pattern for a leak harness.
  - vectorbt's licence adds the Commons Clause and its purged CV is paid, so skip it.
- **Binance metrics files** changed the meaning of `create_time` on 2026-06-25 according to an issue in the
  binance-public-data repository (UNVERIFIED here). The open interest loader should be checked for a one-period
  shift before anything reads it.
