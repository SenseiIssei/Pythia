# pythia-lab

Research for Pythia: where a model or strategy has to prove itself before it
gets near the engine. Runs on the VPS next to the data the recorder writes.
Masterplan phases 4 and 6.

## Run an experiment

```bash
ssh pythia-vps
```

```bash
docker run --rm --cpus 4 --memory 8g -v /srv/pythia-data:/data -v /opt/pythia-lab:/app pythia-lab python -m lab.experiments.vol
```

The image holds only the dependencies; the code is bind-mounted from
`/opt/pythia-lab`, so a change is a copy, not a rebuild. Reports land in
`/srv/pythia-data/reports/<name>/` as dated markdown and JSON plus `latest.*`,
and reach the PC with the next sync.

On the PC, against the synced mirror (which must not be written into), point
the reports elsewhere and copy the markdown into the repo:

```bash
PYTHIA_DATA=F:/PythiaData PYTHIA_REPORTS=F:/PythiaData/agent-models/reports PYTHIA_RESULTS=results F:/PythiaData/venv-net/Scripts/python.exe -m lab.experiments.xs_daily
```

`results/<name>.md` holds the latest report of each experiment run that way.
`xs_daily` caches its panel and scores under `$PYTHIA_REPORTS/_cache`
(`--refresh` rebuilds the panel, `--refit` all models, `--refit-net` only the
funding-aware round-three models); `regime` reads that
panel for its breadth features, so run `xs_daily` first.

## The rules every experiment follows

- **Out of sample or it did not happen.** Walk-forward by quarter with an
  embargo (`cv.py`); labels that span a split are purged (`walk_forward`'s
  `horizon_h` or `label_end_us`, since T2; before it the embargo alone did it).
- **Costs are in every number.** A backtest without costs is not reported.
- **Count the tries.** Every variant tried goes into the deflated Sharpe
  (`metrics.py`); picking the best of many is how noise becomes a strategy.
  Since R5 one append-only trial registry, `<data>/trials/registry.jsonl`
  (`PYTHIA_TRIALS_REGISTRY`), holds every trial: the hand studies' 1,377 as
  the results files counted them (blocks at the variance backed out of the
  published bar, 2.43 over 377), S1's 61 configurations, S2's 8 and L1's 1,
  and every genome of the weekly factory and of F2 the moment it is
  evaluated. `metrics.trial_pool(family)` gives N and the variance of trial
  Sharpes by family (`hand`, `factory`, `small`) or in all;
  `metrics.deflated_sharpe` reads them when no count is passed. One
  convention everywhere: the DSR, higher is better, pass at 0.95 or more,
  over the pool of all (the family's shown next to it). pump.fun's per-entry
  Sharpes do not pool with daily ones and stay outside.
- **Nothing from the future, checked by a machine.** Since T1 `lab.leak` runs a
  function on its data and on copies where every value after t is shuffled,
  shifted, missing or cut, for a sample of t; anything at or before t that
  moves is a leak (`results/leak_harness_prereg.md`). The factories reject a
  genome (`factory/leaks.jsonl`) or spec (G2 `holdout.jsonl`, status `leak`)
  that fails before its held-back look; the weekly job `leak-check` covers
  every feature, the zoo's feature builders, the G2 shadows and the paper
  books' weight functions (`reports/leak/latest.md`, a red line in the health
  check per failure).
- **Measure the overfitting, not only the count.** Since T2
  (`results/overfit_yardsticks_prereg.md`, `results/overfit_yardsticks.md`):
  PBO by CSCV (`metrics.cscv`, 16 blocks, every split) over each factory's
  whole family with the rank logits' distribution (weekly factory and F2 on
  their ledgers, G2 per target); a combinatorial purged CV (`cv.cpcv`) of every
  candidate that reaches validation, the median and 10th percentile of its
  paths; one line per candidate in the reports (DSR with its direction, PBO,
  CPCV). Hansen's SPA test and the model confidence set (`metrics.spa`,
  `metrics.mcs`, stationary bootstrap) for the zoo and M4 against their
  baselines. The zoo replaces a champion only when SPA rejects "no better than
  the champion" at 5 %, and a model that failed its gate is not retrained
  until a book reads it (`python -m lab.models.train --replay 30` replays the
  last days under both rules).
- **One ladder to real money.** Since T3 `lab.ops.ladder`
  (`results/readiness_ladder_prereg.md`) puts every paper book, factory
  survivor and shadow on a step: 1 idea (pre-registered before its first
  number), 2 tested (leak check, one close late, costs of their era, DSR over
  the registry and the family, PBO), 3 shadow (30 days), 4 lockbox (R6's look
  against the right benchmark), 5 execution (50 EUR under the review's rules),
  6 larger (only the owner). `reports/ladder/latest.md`, written by the Lab Live
  job; the briefing's "Stufenleiter" card and each book's node show the step.
  It only proposes. The readiness drawdown is on cash-flow-adjusted equity.
  Since A4 (`results/ladder_promotion_a4_prereg.md`, job `promote`,
  `lab.ops.promote`) steps 2 to 4 are automatic: a candidate on step 2 gets
  one forward shadow from its own record (`<data>/promotions/shadows.json`),
  after 30 days and 30 trades or rebalances one lockbox look (claimed and
  registered first, a second refused, a fail final), and the passes go to
  `<data>/promotions/live_ready.json` with a share of 50 EUR. Kinds that cannot
  run yet (G2's models; an F2 genome without its wallet; the lockbox of books
  and F2) show "missing tool" on the ladder. Paper only.
- **Beat a baseline, not zero.** Naive and HAR for volatility, the coin
  basket and BTC for strategies, random entries for signals.
- **Write down failures.** A dead idea with a number is a result.

### Forward report

`lab.paper.forward_report` (06:15 UTC, see `research/ops/README.md`) asks of
every paper book and every autopilot of the autopilot engine whether the
forward result is what the backtest led us to expect, in one plain sentence
each: "on track", "below the backtest's range after N days, worth watching",
"too early to say" (under 7 days), and so on. Report:
`reports/forward/latest.{md,json}`; the Lab page shows it as "Forward test".

- **Band.** A moving-block bootstrap (5-day blocks, 4000 draws) of the
  backtest's out-of-sample daily net returns, compounded over the same
  number of days the book has run: 5 % to 95 %, median in the middle, plus
  the 95 % quantile of the worst dip inside such a stretch. The series are
  `reports/forward/backtest/<book>.json`, written by `--export-backtests`
  (momentum books from the history files, sweep candidates from the sweep's
  run caches at 15 bps, M4 from the xs_daily holdout at 15 bps with funding).
  Without one the band is log-normal from the report's growth and
  volatility, marked "approximate" (the M2 books, which have only weekly
  report rows).
- **What it can prove (T8).** `lab.paper.power` after each run: per book and
  factory shadow the days needed to show its validated Sharpe (5 %, 80 %
  power, fixed and sequential), an e-value for an edge and one for a fall
  below validation (either reaching 20 on any day decides, valid under daily
  looks), a pool per family; `reports/forward/power.{md,json}` and a section
  in the report. `evidence.json` does not carry it.
- **Costs.** Summed `paper_cost_bps` against `model_cost_bps`; the M2 books
  journal the modelled 10 bps as their fill cost, so they say so instead of
  a ratio. Funding where a book has it.
- **Gate 7.** Journal days and rebalances (rows with turnover over 1 %), 30
  each; for autopilots days running and closed trades.
- **Autopilots.** From `PYTHIA_AUTOPILOT_STATE_URL` (default
  `http://127.0.0.1:8788/api/state`, `off` to skip): equity against start and
  floor, return against BTC bought and held over the same window (Binance
  1-minute opens), fees as a share of the gain before fees, trades per day
  (from one day on), and realised against modelled slippage per route from
  the orders the engine still keeps. An autopilot that runs a single lab book
  is held against that book's band. Return and fees are measured on the
  amount it worked with (the engine's time-weighted `avgCapital`), so a
  changed amount turns no dollars into a bigger or smaller return.
- **Frozen records.** A book in `FROZEN` (the forward audit's kill rule)
  counts nothing up to its cut date; its fresh record starts with the next
  run, from the equity at the cut, and its sentence and evidence say why.

`lab.paper.forward_audit` checks the records themselves (pre-registered in
`results/forward_audit_prereg.md`): every journal row of the daily books
against its backtest rerun from the history files, the forward return among
the backtest's same-length windows at 35 and 70 bps, and the realised cost of
every autopilot fill in the engine's saved states. On the PC:
`PYTHIA_DATA=F:/PythiaData PYTHIA_REPORTS=<elsewhere> PYTHIA_RESULTS=results python -m lab.paper.forward_audit`
(its panels are built under the reports folder, never in the data folder).

  migrated pool, and gaps, as daily parquet under `<data>/pumpfun_rt/`
  (`store.py`, size budget). Not PumpPortal (token trades need a key tied to a
  funded wallet), not pump.fun's own API (its terms forbid bots).
- **Chain time.** The endpoint delivers a third of the trades more than a
  second late and some out of order, so everything is ordered by slot and
  timed by the slot (the earliest receive time of that slot or a later one),
  never by an event's own receive time.
- **Fills** (`curve.py`, `sim.py`): our order lands 0.4 to 3 s after the
  signal (lognormal, median 1 s), at the curve after every trade before it,
  with the token's fee (1.25 %), 0.001 SOL priority fee and the curve's own
  slippage; a buy fails above 30 % slippage or on a completed curve; a sale
  takes whatever the curve or the pool pays when it lands. The study's
  replays keep these registered assumptions.
- **Realistic paper** (`realistic.py`, R1, job `pumpfun-rt-realistic`): the
  pipeline paper books on the live stream are executed the way the live bot
  is: its buy and sale latency drawn per trade from its last 7 days of
  orders (refitted daily), fills at the first recorded trade after the
  landing, the live pool bound with the pre-send skip and 6040, its failure
  rates, fees and Sender tip, and its sizing on a 0.55 SOL paper wallet per
  book; the old books as `<name>_ideal` until 2026-11-06, and a daily line
  "live vs realistic paper" for P_pool0_wait (`reports/pumpfun_rt/realistic.md`).
  Since R2 (`labels_r2.py`) the models train on labels made the same way (the
  study's `<label>_r` columns, the select label book through the realistic
  executor), the old labels' models as `_ideal` until 2026-11-07.
- **Factory** (`factory.py`, F3, job `pumpfun-factory`, 01:00): a nightly
  Optuna search over strategies and the LightGBM settings of the realistic
  labels, every trial a realistic book with a 0.55 SOL wallet bootstrap,
  every trial in `<data>/pumpfun_rt/factory/ledger.jsonl` and in the
  deflated bar, the last 2 days held back; winners become paper books
  F3_<id> (at most 5, retired after 14 days behind P_pool5_top30t_sw10o3n,
  a proposal for the owner after 14 days ahead), never live
  (`reports/pumpfun_rt/factory.md`).
- **Model zoo and allocator** (A1, `zoo.py`, `selector.py`, `allocator.py`,
  in the daily retrain): LightGBM (with F3's settings), logistic regression,
  scikit-learn's histogram boosting, an online logistic regression and a
  competing-risks hazard model behind one interface, walked forward by day
  on R2's labels; per label the selector's pick (5 days ahead beyond the
  noise band to take over, retired after 7 days behind the logistic one,
  isotonic out of fold) and the winner per market regime
  (`reports/pumpfun_rt/zoo.md`); no book reads it yet. An allocator over
  P_pool0_wait, P_pool0_wait_lock and P_pool5_top30t on their realistic
  paper days splits the paper wallet by each one's posterior probability of
  being best (T9, instead of A1's one Thompson draw: every arm runs as paper,
  so every reward is seen anyway; an entry shared by k arms counts 1 / k in
  each; floor 5 %, drawdown guard 30 %) and names the largest weight
  (`reports/pumpfun_rt/allocator.json`, the card); never the live bot.
- **Rules** (`strategies.py`): 22 pre-registered rules in five families
  (snipe at creation, first-seconds pressure, momentum, pre-migration,
  post-migration) plus the model filter, 23 trials in the global pool.
- **Model** (`model.py`): features at 3 s to 5 min after creation, labels
  "creator dumps within 60 s", "completes within 6 h", "a paper round trip
  over 30 s / 5 min wins after costs"; LightGBM, isotonic calibration, purged
  walk-forward against the base rate and a logistic baseline; retrained daily
  into `<data>/models/pumpfun_rt/`.
- **Daily study** (`study.py`, job `pumpfun-rt-study`): both passes, the bar,
  `reports/pumpfun_rt/latest.json` (lab feed header), `pick.json` for the
  shadow book, and a daily card in English or German. Big paper winners

## Strategy factory (W10)

`python -m lab.factory` (weekly on the server, see `research/ops/README.md`)
invents long-only daily strategies and holds them to the hand studies' bar.

- **Grammar** (`factory/grammar.py`). A genome is universe (top N by
  liquidity, the core 20, Alpaca's 15), signal (momentum, Donchian, MA cross,
  short-term reversal, low vol), up to three filters (BTC 200-day or
  momentum gate, vol cap, volume confirmation, coin above its own average),
  selection (best K by signal, liquidity or low vol), weights (equal or
  inverse vol, empty slots in cash or the whole book spread) and rebalance
  (daily, every 3 days, weekly, weekly in 7 tranches; no-trade band 0, 25 or
  50 %). At most 7 moving parts; each costs 0.03 of fitness.
- **Expression trees (G1).** The signal, a coin filter or a gate may also be
  a small tree the factory invents: series close, volume, range ((high - low)
  / close), annualised vol and BTC's close; operators return over n, moving
  average ratio and z-score over n (n from 3, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200),
  cross-sectional rank within the universe, sign, clip, difference, ratio,
  min and max; since C2 also candle shapes (`build.candle`, the open is the
  close before since the panel has none): body, upper and lower wick as
  shares of the day's range, the same for the last 7 days as one candle and
  that candle's range over the close (`body_w`, `upper_w`, `lower_w`,
  `range_w`; a range ratio is `ma_n(range)`);
  depth at most 3, at most 9 nodes; close, volume and BTC only
  inside a window operator; a gate is made of BTC alone. Mutation (a window,
  an operator, a leaf, a new or hoisted subtree, a wrap) and subtree
  crossover; a canonical form, so equal trees are one key and one trial. A
  tree counts its nodes as moving parts (at most 14 in a tree genome) and
  costs 0.05 of fitness a node (0.03 plus 0.02: an invented block needs more
  evidence). Every operator reads only bars up to the one it scores
  (`tests/test_factory_trees.py` replaces every bar after t and checks that
  no score at or before t moves). Reports write a tree readably, e.g.
  `rank(ret_20) where btc > ma_100`. Genomes without a tree keep their keys,
  so the ledger's old rows stay the same trials. Since D3 a leaf may also be
  a feature of the feature store (`f.funding_8h`, see "Feature store"); the
  weekly search draws those with data over half of its in-sample window.
- **Search** (`factory/search.py`). A fixed budget of new genomes per run
  (300 by default), half random on the first run and a third afterwards, the
  rest mutation and crossover of the best ever tried. 30 % of the slots are
  tree genomes (`PYTHIA_FACTORY_TREE_SHARE` or `--tree-share`, the same for the
  small mode), the rest the old families; each kind breeds from its own
  elite. It sees only 2018
  to 2023 at 35 bps, on the panel with Binance's daily bars before 2020
  (`lab.history`, D1; until then 2020-08 to 2023, rows of that window stay
  trials in the pool but are not bred from or held back). A fixed list is scored by the worse of the list and its
  point-in-time twin (the same number of the most liquid coins at the start
  of each year), so survivorship cannot win.
- **Ledger** (`factory/ledger.py`). Every genome ever evaluated is one trial
  in `<data>/factory/ledger.jsonl`, on top of the 377 hand-study trials (bar
  Sharpe 2.43); the deflation pool only grows. `results/factory_seed/` holds
  what was counted elsewhere (the first run was made on the PC) and every run
  merges it first.
- **Gates** (`factory/evaluate.py`). Six genomes per run (best fitness, both
  in-sample halves positive, mutually uncorrelated) see 2024 onwards: Sharpe
  above 0 at 35 bps and at 70 bps, drawdown below BTC's, still positive when
  filled a close late, and for a fixed list its twin positive too. A survivor
  runs 30 days as a forward shadow book (`factory/shadow.py`, at most 6 at a
  time, 2 new per run). After 30 shadow days it is promoted only if every
  gate holds again, deflated p is above 0.95 over the whole pool and the
  forward month is not below the backtest's 5 % quantile; it then becomes a
  paper book (`factory_<key>`) stepped by `lab.paper.families` with live
  quotes. Nothing the factory does can reach live trading.
- **Report.** `reports/factory/latest.{md,json}` (JSON with the common header:
  title, updatedMs, status, headline, items) and, with `--notify`, a digest
  to Telegram or the webhook through `lab.ops.notify.post`.

## Model factory (G2)

`python -m lab.models.factory` (daily on the server, job `model-factory`)
makes, tests and rotates prediction models by itself (`models/factory.py`,
next to M1's learners). Paper only.

- **Specs.** A target (M8's next-day direction, M10's next-day realised
  variance, M12's 20-day BTC and SPY correlation, or the strategy factory's
  forward return: the next 7 days' log return over the coin's 30-day vol
  scaled to 7 days, less
  the day's cross-sectional mean, on the 15 Alpaca coins), a feature subset
  of the zoo's builders (M8's and M10's inputs, M12's for M12) with up to two
  of G1's expression trees on the coin targets (evaluated by
  `factory.build.tree_values` on the panel pivoted to day and coin), a
  learner (LightGBM from a small space, ridge, logistic regression; no MLP,
  torch is not in the lab image) and a training window (1, 2, 3 years or all).
  Random (the first run, then a third) or a mutation of a target's best.
- **Screen.** Quarterly walk-forward 2021 to 2023, expanding, purged (a
  training row's label ends before the test block starts: 1 day for M8 and
  M10, 7 for the forward return, 32 calendar days for M12), against the
  trivial baseline (base rate, HAR, the last 60 days' correlation, 0) and the
  zoo model it would replace, fitted by its own module in the same folds.
  Fitness is the smaller of the two gains in %.
- **Ledger and bar.** Every spec is one trial in
  `<data>/models/factory/ledger.jsonl`, keyed by a hash of its settings.
  At most two screen passes a run are held back on 2024-01 to 2026-06 (R6's
  lockbox stays untouched), each look written to `holdout.jsonl` first; a
  pass needs a lower loss than the baseline and the incumbent with a DM p
  under 0.05 / N, N every trial in the ledger. Losses are not Sharpes, so
  these trials stay out of the trial registry.
- **Shadows.** A pass joins the zoo as a shadow member (at most 2 a run, 6 at
  a time): refitted daily, its forecast logged before the outcome and scored
  against the baseline and the zoo's logged forecasts; after 30 scored days
  the allocator may pick it (a Thompson draw per target over the incumbent
  and the eligible members, `picks.json`, for paper books only); 60 days
  behind the baseline or the incumbent retire it.
- **Run.** A time budget (`PYTHIA_MODEL_FACTORY_BUDGET`, seconds), resumable;
  `results/model_factory_seed/` (the first run's trials) is merged first;
  `reports/models/factory.{md,json}`, one line in the briefing's research
  factory card. `tests/test_model_factory_g2.py` replaces every row after a
  day by noise and checks that no forecast, baseline, incumbent, tree input
  or known loss before it moves. First run: `results/model_factory_g2.md`.

## Feature store (D3)

`lab.features` is one point-in-time source for every feature a model or a
factory may read, the recorder's order books included.

- **Registry** (`features/registry.py`). A feature has a name, the raw
  sources it reads, their native frequency, the delay after which a live
  system knows it, and a function on its group's hourly or daily table.
  `features.frame(names, assets, times, "1h" | "1d")` gives at each time the
  newest value known by then (end of its bucket by `ts_recv_us`, plus the
  delay) and not older than one bucket (or the feature's own max age), so
  nothing later than "known at" reaches a model.
- **Microstructure** (`features/micro.py`), hourly and daily: Binance spot's
  and Kraken's quoted spread, notional within 10 and 50 bp of the mid (within
  the 20 recorded levels), the 50 bp book imbalance and its change; the
  dispersion of the five venues' minute mids (USDT books in USD at Kraken's
  USDT/USD); Kraken's taker flow imbalance, notional and realised vol from
  1-minute trade prices; Binance USD-M funding as published, the mark's
  premium, open interest change (the 5-minute archive from 2022, the
  recorder after it), liquidations by side (a quiet hour is 0).
- **Other series** (`features/external.py`): settled funding of every perp
  from 2020, Deribit's BTC DVOL and 25-delta skew, DefiLlama's stablecoin
  supply, the Coinbase premium; the D2 files (Fear and Greed, Hyperliquid
  funding, macro closes, an event calendar) when they exist, empty columns
  until then.
- **Order book depth** (`features/book.py`, T4), hourly and daily for every
  perp of `lab.collect.book_depth` from 2023 (Binance's `bookDepth` archive):
  `bd_depth02_usd` and `bd_depth1_usd` (bid plus ask notional within 0.2 % and
  1 % of mid; 0.2 % from 2026-01-16), `bd_imb02`, `bd_imb1`, their change
  `bd_depth02_chg`, `bd_depth1_chg`, `bd_depth1_chg_24h`, and `bd_cost10k_bps`,
  `bd_cost100k_bps` (mean slippage from mid of a market order, buy and sell
  averaged, linear between the levels). Known at the bucket's end; the archive
  publishes a day 7 to 10 h after it (`published_us` in the rows). The same
  module keeps a point-in-time delisting table (announcements and the
  archive's stops); `universe.tradable(P, coins, drop_delisting=True)` drops a
  coin from the day its delisting was announced.
- **Cache** (`features/store.py`). The hourly tables of every finished UTC
  day as `<data>/features/<group>/date=*/hourly.parquet`, rebuilt only when
  a day is new or its raw files changed; the nightly job `features`
  (`python -m lab.features build`) also writes `coverage.json`. Raw
  retention is unchanged, the cache keeps the days the raw books lose.
- **Factories** (`features/adapter.py`). `model_frame(names, rows)` is the
  matrix a model would read (G2's model factory reads the zoo's daily panels). The strategy
  factory's expression trees take any feature as a leaf `f.<name>` (a dollar
  amount like a level, a market feature like `btc`); the weekly search draws
  those defined daily whose coverage spans half of its in-sample window (today
  `funding_8h`, `stables_chg7`, `coinbase_prem_bps`), the books' features
  start when the recorder did and wait until they do.
- **Checks.** `tests/test_features.py` computes every feature by hand on a
  tiny recorded sample (two hours of the recorder's BTC and ETH rows),
  changes every raw row after t and checks that nothing at or before t
  moves, hourly and daily. `python -m lab.features report` writes coverage
  and rank IC against the next hour and the next day with the tests counted
  (`results/feature_store_d3.md`).

## Candles (C1)

`lab.candles.store.candles(asset, timeframe, start, end)` is one call for
bars of every market Pythia looks at, at 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1d and 1w
(UTC buckets, weeks from Monday): open, high, low, close, volume, quote
volume, trades, taker buy share, VWAP, the source and `complete`. A bar is
built from the finest layer there is for its span and only from sub-bars
closed by `as_of` (default now); a bar still forming is never complete.

- **Binance spot** (default for the lab's coins and PAXG): the recorder's
  1m archive from 2020, then the 5m, 1h and 1d archives where it has no 1m
  (daily from 2017). The archive lags about a day, so job `candles-live`
  (every 10 minutes, `python -m lab.candles.live live`) fills the newest
  days from the public klines REST endpoint (no key).
- **Kraken**, a second source: 1m candles from the recorded trades (buy and
  sell volume by the taker's side, VWAP), built by the same job; each
  minute's close against Binance's is `ref_dev_bps`, above 0.5 % it is
  flagged (`disagreements()`). The candles stay when the raw trades go.
- **Solana**: the xStocks tokens S2 and F2 paper (SPYx, QQQx, GLDx, NVDAx),
  PAXG and the on-chain coins' deepest USDC or SOL pool on GeckoTerminal's
  free API, 1h and 1d in USD with the pool's liquidity; Yahoo 1d (from 2000)
  and 1h (two years) of the underlyings SPY, QQQ, GLD, NVDA. Job
  `candles-markets` (hourly; one call per 6 s, and with job `pumpfun`'s
  calls, which share the keyless quota, one per 7.5 s across processes
  (`lab.hostpace`, a file lock under `<write root>/ratelimit/`; since
  2026-10-29, when the two together got 429 after 4 calls an hour), at
  most 40 calls and 5 minutes a run; pools without a bar first, then where the last run stopped
  (`gecko_cursor.json`), then the stalest, the 1h history walked back half a
  year last; a 429 waits once, the next ends the run, keeps the rest for the
  next one and backs it off from 2 up to 30 minutes). `candles-health` is
  red per pool: each one whose 1h bar is over 3 hours or 1d bar over 2 days
  old, or that has none, is named. Markets and limits: `lab/candles/markets.json`.
- **Storage** under `<data>/candles/source=<s>/asset=<a>/tf=<tf>/`: a
  month's parts, merged into `<YYYY-MM>.parquet` by job `candles-compact`
  (01:40 UTC, finished months final), `manifest.json` (per market and
  timeframe: first and last bar, rows, months), `state.json`. The same job
  writes the gap report (`python -m lab.candles.store report`,
  `results/candle_coverage_c1.md`). Job `candles-health` is red, and so is
  the briefing's health line, when the REST fill or the Kraken candles are
  more than 30 minutes old or the pools more than 3 hours.
- **Features**: `lab.features` reads the coins' 1h and 1d bars as
